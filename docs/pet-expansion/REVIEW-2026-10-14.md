# Pet skill review — 2026-10-14

## Implemented changes

- Pet rows reuse character skill backgrounds, group icons, level badges and active cooldown sweeps/countdown digits. Timer redraw continues without incoming packets. Unlearned, unavailable and cooling-down skills cannot be activated.
- Server publishes changes to attack/hit/kill recharge counters. The activating strike does not immediately consume its own new recharge requirement.
- Blocked strikes cannot execute enemies or advance damaging-attack passives. Execute damage is assigned to a nonblocked impact.
- Gear damage/healing procs obey the configured internal cooldown and choose at most one proc per strike.
- Cold Mastery modifies magical attack. Last Stand's second rank uses the 40% HP threshold. Retribution records post-mitigation damage, capped to available pet HP.
- Gear replacement/removal rejects a nonzero inventory remainder.
- Registered the existing pet state owner and its execution callbacks in client architecture manifests.
- Updated the canonical workbook and regenerated all three consumers. Corrected misleading leap, spell echo, Myopia, freeze, healing and defense descriptions. Unimplemented advanced ranks explicitly say "Planned, not active".
