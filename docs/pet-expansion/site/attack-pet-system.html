Attack Pet System

Four companions. One shared equipment system.

Build a partner for your journey: a stalwart bear, a relentless wolf, a frost-wielding penguin or a supporting crow.

Command active abilities yourself, develop passive skills and find pet equipment on the monsters you defeat. Each companion has its own role and five skill families to develop.

This guide describes the optional attack-pet expansion, not the unchanged original pet system. It must be enabled by the server. Numbers below reflect the current design, reviewed 16 October 2026.

Getting started

  1. Summon an attack pet from its summon item. The expansion supports Bear, Wolf, Penguin and Three Footed Crow; it does not turn pickup pets into attack pets.
  2. Open pet information with Insert or the Information button beside the pet portrait.
  3. Check the tabs. Basic Info shows the pet's combat stats; Inventory holds its equipment; Skills shows its training tree.
  4. Learn a skill when your pet meets the level, previous-book and skill-point requirements. Unmet requirements appear red in the tooltip.
  5. Drag a learned active skill to a normal numbered quickslot. Press that slot's key to command the currently summoned pet.

Missing Inventory or Skills? Check Experimental → Game → Attack-pet training in the client. Both the client option and the server expansion must be enabled.

Choose your companion

Equipment is shared across species, but their underlying combat profiles differ. These are roles, not a promise of identical stats at the same level.

Bear · Defense

Hold the line

High health and strong defenses, with lower attack. Draw enemies away from your character and withstand their attacks.

Bear skill families
  • Active · Challenging RoarTaunts nearby monsters without dealing damage. Book I starts with three normal monsters in an 8 m base radius; II admits party monsters and starts at 12 m; III raises the limit to six and starts at 16 m. Proficiency increases radius. Uniques are excluded.
  • Active · Iron HideCommand an immediate, short damage-reduction buff. No selected monster or basic attack is needed.
  • Passive · Thick HideIncreases physical and magical defense.
  • Passive · Last StandRaises defense while health is low; higher books improve its protection.
  • Passive · Crushing PawCounts attacks toward a periodic stunning strike. Higher proficiency improves the effect.

Wolf · Physical attack

Hunt down your prey

A physical damage specialist. Bleeding, wounded targets and finishing blows reward sustained pressure.

Wolf skill families
  • Active · Terrifying HowlTerrifies a limited number of nearby monsters, preventing them from attacking the wolf for a short time. It is not a boss-wide fear.
  • Active · Rending BiteA commanded damaging strike that applies bleeding. Select a valid enemy first.
  • Passive · ExecuteA damaging hit can finish a non-unique monster below its remaining-health threshold. Blocked hits do not execute.
  • Passive · Scent of BloodImproves critical chance against bleeding or wounded enemies.
  • Passive · Thick FurIncreases physical and magical defense.

Penguin · Magic

Control the battlefield

Strong magical offense with less health and physical defense. Magical attack improves its damage; defensive gear helps it survive.

Penguin skill families
  • Active · Frost NovaA commanded area strike that attempts frostbite on nearby targets. Native resistance rules determine status success.
  • Active · Deep FreezeA targeted commanded strike that attempts freeze. Duration and resistance depend on the pet and target; bosses have no guaranteed slow fallback.
  • Passive · Cold MasteryIncreases magical attack.
  • Passive · Static FeathersChance to spread an attack to additional enemies with reduced damage and an electric-shock attempt. It does not cast a separate chain-lightning spell.
  • Passive · Frost ArmorIncreases physical and magical defense. It does not automatically chill attackers.

Crow · Support

Keep your journey going

Supports its owner with healing and enemy debuffs. Its current speed passive improves the crow's attacks, not party movement.

Crow skill families
  • Active · MendCommand an immediate heal based on the owner's maximum health.
  • Active · Mark PreyA commanded strike attempts to reduce the target's physical and magical defense. Recharge counts attacks rather than seconds.
  • Passive · Carrion FeastHeals the owner when the crow lands the killing blow.
  • Passive · Swift WingsIncreases the crow's attack speed. Owner and party movement speed are unchanged.
  • Passive · Murder of CrowsPeriodic damaging attacks strike nearby enemies. Uses the crow's existing attack animation.

Hover a skill in game for its current and next-rank values. Some higher-book bonuses change behavior as well as numbers; the in-game description is the reference for your pet's proficiency.

Three books in every row

Each pet has two active rows and three passive rows. The three icons in a row are successive books of the same skill family, not three unrelated abilities.

Each book has ten learnable ranks. Its next rank may need a higher pet level even when that book is already unlocked. The tooltip shows the exact requirement.

A pet starts with one training point and earns another every two pet levels. Points are shared across all five rows: you must choose where to invest. Special test pets may have extra points; those are not normal progression.

Only one book contributes from each passive row at a time. Learning a higher passive book selects it automatically; earlier books do not add a second copy of the same bonus.

Commands, quickslots & cooldowns

  • You control active skills. Bind them while ready, then use a numbered quickslot just like a character skill. The summoned pet must know the bound skill.
  • Offensive skills need a valid target. Self-buffs, healing and area commands have their own admission rules; a roar needs eligible monsters in range.
  • All books in one active row share recharge. Casting Book III also puts Books I and II in that row on cooldown. The other active row remains independent.
  • Timed recharge shows a sweep and countdown in the skill tree and quickslot. The skill becomes available when its timer expires.
  • Event recharge counts actions such as attacks or hits taken. Waiting does not clear an attack counter. Read the tooltip to distinguish a counter from seconds.

Cooldown reduction has a ceiling

Equipment blues and applicable passives add together, then the total is capped at 80%. The effective total is shown in the pet Inventory tab.

Remaining timed cooldown = base cooldown × (1 − effective reduction)

The 150% row explains the cap, not a promise that any three items can roll cooldown reduction. Currently that blue is eligible on harnesses and charms. Event-based recharge uses rounded counters with a minimum of one event.

Equipment found on the road

Attack pets share three equipment slots. A piece can be used by any supported species that meets its pet-level requirement.

Mounting partBase statsUseful for
CollarPhysical attack, magical attack, hit ratioOffense and accuracy
HarnessPhysical defense, magical defense, parry ratioSurviving attacks
CharmSmaller bonuses to all six statsRounding out your build

White tooltip lines are base equipment stats. Blue lines are rolled modifiers, such as percentage damage or defense, cooldown reduction, elemental additions or extra skill levels. A plain item does not secretly grant those percentage bonuses.

Find pet gear from monsters, not NPC shops. When a monster produces an eligible normal character-equipment drop, the server may add a pet item of the same degree. The original character item remains. Gold and potions do not trigger this extra roll.

The default factor is 10% per eligible equipment drop: about ten additional pet items per hundred such drops over time, not a 10% chance on every kill. Server operators can change this rate.

Pick up the item, check its requirement, then drag it from your inventory to the matching pet slot. Right-click a worn piece to remove it when permitted. Recall the pet and leave combat before changing gear or training if the server refuses the action.

Nine degrees, stronger possibilities

Degrees follow character equipment bands through 9D. A monster can roll a lower-degree normal item, so its pet drop can also be below that monster's highest eligible degree.

A maximum is not a guarantee. Plain items can have zero blues. Higher degrees improve roll floors and unlock stronger eligible tiers, but a 9D item can still roll a lower tier or fewer than six options.

Each modifier group can appear only once on an item. A blue must also match the slot and degree. Higher percentages are deliberately rarer; the server controls each tier's limits and roll bias. Saved item rolls do not change when future drop settings change.

Care for your companion

  • Watch HP and HGP. Low satiety can lower combat performance, so feed the pet before comparing equipment or skill damage.
  • A pet above its owner's level cannot be summoned normally. Keep the owner eligible while training.
  • Gear, learned ranks and passive selections are saved with the pet's summon item. Relogging does not grant a fresh build.
  • Recharge state is saved too. Timers can expire as time passes; event counters still need their specified actions.
  • Use weak enemies when trying a new build. A skill icon working does not make an underfed or poorly equipped pet safe against high-level monsters.

Common questions

Why can I not learn the next skill?

Check pet level, free points, previous-book rank and combat state. Book II needs Book I maxed; Book III needs Book II maxed. Unmet tooltip requirements are red, even on unlearned skills.

Why does another skill icon go dark when I cast?

All three books in that row share a cooldown. It prevents cycling through the same family to avoid recharge.

Why is Mark Prey not recharging while I wait?

Its recharge is attack-based. Make the required pet attacks; it is not a timed countdown.

Do stronger blues change the white numbers on the item?

No. The item's white lines describe its fixed base stats. Rolled percentage effects remain blue; the pet's Basic Info reflects applicable equipment and passive combat modifiers.

Does 9D guarantee six blues or a 50% cooldown roll?

No. Six is its blue cap. Each roll still chooses an eligible group and tier, then a biased value within the allowed range. The top cooldown tier is currently D9 harness/charm gear; its configured maximum is not a guaranteed value.

Does this work on the public server already?

This is a guide for the optional expansion build. Seeing this page does not mean every server has enabled it. Check that server's announcements and the pet tabs in game.
