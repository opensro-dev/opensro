AUDITED OPERATION
Clear active PK
REQUIRES RELOGIN

    

     
Clears the inspected character's active PK penalty and aggression on the displayed server. Daily and total kill history are retained. Their active session will be disconnected. Ask the player to log in again afterward.

     
     
     
     
The original state and result are recorded in the private audit log. This operation does not select a town or grant items.

    

   

   

    
AUDITED OPERATION
Reset stats
REQUIRES RELOGIN

    

     
Returns the inspected character's STR and INT to their value at the current level and gives every spent point back as a free stat point. No earned point is lost. Health and mana above the new maximum are trimmed. Their active session will be disconnected. Ask the player to log in again afterward.

     
     
     
     
The original state and result are recorded in the private audit log. This operation does not move the character or grant items.
